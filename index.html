What was approved, drawn against what was observed.

AquaSentinel reads three registers from a fictional water utility (assets, approval windows and imported observations) and draws every place they disagree. Each mark on the sheet below is placed from a timestamp in the synthetic golden fixture. Nothing is scanned, fetched or connected.

01 · Reading the sheet

Two layers, never merged.

The register layer is what the utility's records say should exist. The imported layer is what an observation file says was seen. AquaSentinel compares them. It does not reconcile them into one picture, and it never edits either.

Approved path
An approval record joins an asset to a path ID. Only P1 (A1 to V1) and P2 (A2 to V2) are on record.
Approval window
validFrom to validThrough, with both endpoints counted as inside.
Imported observation
One row from the observation file, placed at its observedAt.
Observed pairing
An asset and path seen together in an import with no approval on record for that exact pair.
Snapshot and stale zone
The snapshot is 2026-11-03T12:00:00Z. Hatching marks anything more than 86,400 seconds older. Exactly 86,400 seconds does not count as stale.

03 · General notes

Passive by construction.

Every engineering sheet carries its general notes. These are the conditions under which the drawing above means anything.

  1. Import only. This slice has no scanning, URL fetching, DNS lookups, device connections, PLC writes or remediation. URL-like identifiers stay plain text.
  2. A finding describes a disagreement between records. It says nothing about exposure, severity, compromise or safety.
  3. An absent observation is not evidence that anything is in order.
  4. Identifiers are kept exactly as imported, apart from trimming surrounding ASCII whitespace. A collision caused by trimming is rejected. There are no guessed aliases, no inferred ports and no merging by name.
  5. Any validation error rejects the whole import, and the previous dataset stays in place. Reviewer notes and dispositions sit in a separate layer and never rewrite imported evidence.
  6. All data is synthetic, and the rule pack is fictional and illustrative. There is no regulatory, legal or compliance claim of any kind.

04 · Into the workbench

Check the sheet against the engine.

The workbench is a separate surface with paper-ledger styling for long review sessions. It runs entirely in your browser on the text you import.

  1. Open the workbench.
  2. The golden fixture is selected by default. Press Load fixture.
  3. The counts should read 5 observations, 4 flagged and 6 evidence gaps.
  4. Open a finding on O3 to see the imported rows behind each reason.
